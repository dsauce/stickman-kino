# Getting help

1. `node bin/stickman.mjs doctor`, then [docs/troubleshooting.md](docs/troubleshooting.md) and the [FAQ](docs/faq.md).
2. Search [issues](https://github.com/dsauce/stickman-kino/issues), including closed ones.
3. Ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/dsauce/stickman-kino/discussions) (Q&A), or open a bug report with the template.

Show-and-tell is very welcome in Discussions: post your films!
